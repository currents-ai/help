# Sites

ACME.BOT can host your blog as a site. Every site gets its own address on `acme.pub`, and you can serve it on your own domain too.

## Guides

- [Connect your own domain to a site](./connect-your-domain): serve your site at a domain you own, such as `blog.example.com`, through Cloudflare
